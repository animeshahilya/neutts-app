# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Added NeuTTS 2E: reads with feeling, in seven tones. A third the size of the others and about
  three times as fast, which makes it the one to use for long reading.
- Speech now starts playing while the rest is still being made, instead of after all of it.
- NeuTTS can now be chosen as the phone's text-to-speech engine, so reading apps can speak with it.
- Voices are now a dropdown, and the app only offers voices the chosen model can actually use.
- Optionally try the phone's AI accelerator for the decoder.
- Removed the German, Portuguese, Vietnamese and Korean models.

## 1.0.0

First release.

- Speak any text in one of nine built-in voices, entirely on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- Choose a speech model from inside the app and hear a sample of each one before downloading it.
- English, Hindi, German, Portuguese, Vietnamese and Korean.
- Optionally record a voice and have the app speak in it.
- Save what you generate as an audio file.
